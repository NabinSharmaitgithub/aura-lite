# Commercial licensing

AURA Lite is free, open-source software under the **GNU AGPL v3** (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and
[`LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md`](LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md)). For most people that is all you need.

## You do not need a commercial licence to

- use AURA Lite on your own phones, personally or at work
- study, modify, and fork the code
- ship your own build or fork, **as long as you publish its full source under the AGPL**
- run a service built on AURA Lite, **as long as you publish its full source under the AGPL**
- charge money for any of the above. The AGPL allows selling; it only forbids hiding the source.

## You do need a commercial licence to

- ship AURA Lite, or a product built on AURA Lite's code, **without** publishing your source code
- combine AURA Lite's code with proprietary code that you don't want to release under the AGPL
- offer a hosted or embedded version of AURA Lite while keeping your changes private

A commercial licence gives you the same code under terms that let you keep your changes closed.
Licences are granted case by case by the copyright holders.

## How to ask

Open an issue titled "Commercial licensing" or contact the maintainers through their GitHub
profiles. Say what you are building, how you plan to distribute it, and whether you need a single
product licence or a per-seat/per-instance agreement.

## Why this works

Every contributor signs the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA lets the project offer
this commercial option for the whole codebase, not just the parts the current maintainers wrote.
Contributors keep the copyright in their own work.
